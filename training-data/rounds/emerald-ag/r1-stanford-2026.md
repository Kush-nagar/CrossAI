Aff explained that regulations would prevent black markets, but it wasn't clear on the link level on why gamblers wouldn't migrate, especially with addicted or high-risk individuals.
Cartels/extinction - Aff responds with non-unique, but neg defends the causal link that offshore gambling --> organized crime, so I had to buy the magnitude weighing
-your responses were effective to mitigate neg's impacts, but you need to explain more on the link level on especially black markets and the sovereignty rhetoric to prove that FTC regulations would actually outweigh the actual lost revenue
