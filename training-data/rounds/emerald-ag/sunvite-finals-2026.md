## Judge RFD (verbatim)

Granda-Scott, Cecilia
I look to weighing - there are two mechanisms, TF and probability. Aff tells me that 1) recessions have never triggered extinction empirically and 2) war takes a long time to happen because countries need to go into a recession and then build up their militaries to win the diversionary wars. I find both mechanisms compelling. The neg tells me that "diversionary war means no need time to build up", but I'm not really sure why this is true, I think the warrant that militaries want to win in order to successfully divert makes sense. I believe that a stock market crash might happen instantly, but I don't think that means that every part of the link chain happens immediately. Also, I don't think that the neg answers the probability weighing.
I look to China. I actually do think that the idea that China has domestic minerals probably does remove some offense on the argument, but the frontline that "it doesn't take out our arguments because china perceives itself as needing more minerals" is unresponded to so I guess I buy this perceptual argument. At that point, I vote neg.
On the aff flow, I actually do think that the IEA evidence seems to be talking about domestic supply from China? Now, maybe it is the case that by "from China" the IEA means like the percentage of minerals that they produce and they count international supplies as part of that, but I don't think that the evidence indict frontline is fleshed out enough for me to do that work for you. the frontline i do get is that "our second piece of link evidence talks about them getting minerals internationally", but I just get statistics about like what percent of companies in other countries are owned by China, which doesn't prove that China necessarily NEEDS these international areas. Honestly, I don't think whether China needs international resources or not is necessary to win the argument, you could have just said that like "investors being forced to pull out of all these different companies and to stop ongoing projects triggers a collapse in these countries” regardless of whether it hurts China or not” but that's irrelevant at this point.
