## 1. AFF 1st Constructive — [01:36]–[05:09]

- **Three independent ballot routes are planted in one speech**, and only one of
  them is topic-dependent. This is deliberate design: if the K's ontology loses,
  the reparations grievance and (later) the disease IVI survive, because — as the
  aff says explicitly in rebuttal — they are "**not contingent on… ontology**"

- **The Space Traders advocacy is the aff's most exposed piece**, and it is
  exposed in two directions at once: the neg attacks it as **non-topical** (a
  short story, not a legislative instrument — the T violation) *and* as
  **substantively bad on the aff's own terms** ("no freedom fighters,"
  reification, cruel optimism). Reading an advocacy that is simultaneously the T
  violation and the case is an all-in structural choice (−0.5), and the aff
  ultimately **kicks it in final focus** ([46:22]).
- **The reparations position converts an out-of-round grievance into a ballot
  request.** Strategically it is the aff's least-answerable position (the neg
  cannot contest the underlying events on the flow) and its most
  judge-dependent — the ledger's "overinvests in consensus-disliked material…
  expecting the ballot" (−0.6) is the risk, offset by the fact that a **conceded**
  independent voter can be terminal, which is why the neg must and does cover it.

### Round-distinctive execution traits

1. **An out-of-round grievance converted into an in-round ballot request — the
   only instance in this corpus.** The aff's second position asks the judge to
   "surrender your ballot" as a first payment on a debt it says the *tournament*
   owes ([03:41]–[03:59]), with the remedy explicitly unbounded across future

2. **A conceded layer as a deliberate resource, not a loss.** The aff hands the neg
   its entire ontology block ([18:41]) — falsifiability, non-uniqueness, historical

   sentence, and the neg's summary then spends time *banking* a concession that
   buys it nothing on the flows still live.

3. **No grand crossfire, and the 2nd crossfire carries its weight.** Uniquely in

   penultimate exchange, whichever team already banked its cross material keeps

4. **Two teams agreeing on the premise and fighting entirely over the link.** Both
   sides state plainly that racists should lose ([15:28], [30:16], [47:28]); the
   entire back half is a **link race** over whether the neg's model/conduct or the

   heat, unusually well-defined: it is a race into a framing both teams have agreed
   controls (+0.7), with the neg additionally arguing it owns the only procedure
