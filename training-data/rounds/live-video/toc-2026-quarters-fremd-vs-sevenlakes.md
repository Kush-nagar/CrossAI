### Round-distinctive execution traits

1. **A niche policy aff (judicial review of a NATO withdrawal) meeting a
   high-theory K — and the K links to the aff's *solvency mechanism*, not its
   impacts.** The CON's link is not "your impacts are bad reps" but "the very act
   of moving the decision from Trump to Congress assumes humans control weapons"
   [13:24] — an anthropocentrism-of-agency link that the aff's "we say
   unintentional miscalc, not human intentionality" [11:00] answer can't fully
   sever, because the *solvency* still runs through a human at the switch. A clean
   example of a K that attaches to the plan's mechanism rather than its scenario.

2. **"Burden of proof before burden of rejoinder" as the framework crux.** The
   round's hinge is whether the aff must **affirmatively disprove** flat ontology
   (the CON's "you have zero warrants your ontology is right" [45:24]) or whether
   the CON must **prove** its ontology to generate offense (the PRO's
   "self-referential / unpredictable" indict [48:07]). This is the K-round analog
   of who owns the tie — and, as with the UKSO ordering fight, the tape leaves it
   genuinely unresolved.

3. **The K that functions as terminal case defense collapses the usual K
   link-debate.** Because "weapons create desires regardless of who controls them"
   is simultaneously the K's thesis, its link, and its answer to solvency, the CON
   never has to *prove* a separate link — the aff's own plan is the link, and the
   same card zeroes solvency. The lesson mirrors the octos nuclear-K round: when
   the aff's own mechanism *is* the K link, the round moves entirely up to
   framework, and the aff's escape is a softer solvency story (unintentional
   miscalc without a "control" claim) that this aff gestured at but never built.

4. **The evidence layer is where the K is most vulnerable, and the aff found
   it.** Unlike the corpus's Ks that survive on framework alone, this K's terminal
   impact rests on two named cards (growth/night), and the PRO's "read the line"
   indict + "the 500 years is uncarded analysis" is the sharpest aff pressure — a
   reminder that even a framework-heavy K is only as strong as the text under its
   extinction claim, and that forcing the card to be read aloud in cross is the
   move that exposes it.
