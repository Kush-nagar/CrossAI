### Round-distinctive execution traits

1. **A displacement debate, not a clash of unrelated impacts.** Unlike the usual
   two-ships-passing try-or-die round, both cases run the *same* variable —
   where China's resource-seeking goes after the plan. AFF: into cooperation
   (fish joint-development, imports). NEG: into a worse extraction site (Siberia,
   nine-dash oil, an Africa vacuum). That shared mechanism is why the round
   actually interacts: the cooperation frame and the "turns the case" link are
   two readings of one causal question, and the weighing (whose displacement
   fires first) is a genuine comparison rather than a washout.
2. **The round turns on which side actually read its terminal card.** Both
   collapses rest on defense that was *characterized* as much as read, but only
   AFF gets caught: NEG reads an Africa-specific empirical card and then says
   "you never read yours." The single most transferable lesson on the tape is
   the ledger's evidence-integrity ceiling stated negatively — a card you only
   describe ("it's so specific, read it") is, to a flow judge, a card that was
   never in the speech.
3. **Scope-severance beats scope-assertion.** The identical "your card doesn't
   say the specific thing" tactic is run by both firsts, and it wins for whoever
   pairs it with a card they *did* read. NEG quarantines AFF's SCS cooperation
   card off the Africa flow and anchors the sever to its own Africa-specific
   evidence; AFF re-asserts the cooperation card's reach without ever repairing
   the sever. Same move, opposite outcome, decided by evidence discipline.
4. **Clean collapses, contested "concessions."** Both teams make correct
   macro-decisions (drop space; drop Siberia/energy) but paper over the seams
   with inflated drop-claims. The execution gap is not in *what* they collapsed
   to but in whether the "they conceded it" scaffolding under each collapse
   survives a judge who actually flowed the 2nd rebuttal.
