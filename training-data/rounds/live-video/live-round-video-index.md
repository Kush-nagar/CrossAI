# Live Round Video Index

Registry of live Public Forum round recordings transcribed into this corpus.
Each entry points to a per-round transcript + analysis file in this directory
(`training-data/rounds/live-video/`). Source audio stays outside the repo
(local rips, ~35–68 min each) — only transcripts and analysis are stored here.

**24 rounds.** The first eleven (`round-02` … `round-15`) are anonymous
coach-supplied rips and keep numeric slugs; the remaining thirteen are **named
tournament rounds** (TOC, TFA State, Blake, King Round Robin, UKSO, plus one
camp tech-demo) and use descriptive slugs. The named set deliberately widens the
corpus along two dimensions the numeric set barely touched: **kritikal and
progressive debating** (ten rounds — K-affs, negative kritiks, framework/T, pure
disclosure theory, and a philosophy/skepticism round) and **delivery/language
analysis** at the transcript level.

Every round follows standard PF speech order, and each round file segments its
transcript accordingly: 1st Constructive, 2nd Constructive, 1st Crossfire,
1st Rebuttal, 2nd Rebuttal, 2nd Crossfire, 1st Summary, 2nd Summary, Grand
Crossfire, 1st Final Focus, 2nd Final Focus. (One round —
`toc-2025-semis-iowacitywest-vs-strake` — has **no grand crossfire on tape**;
that file flags the gap rather than inventing content for it.)

Analysis in each round file maps observed execution to the drill-feedback
criteria in `reference/factor-weights.md` (the Bayesian-ledger factor rows) and
the `reference/round-vision-framework.md` principles. The cross-round pattern
synthesis at the bottom of this file records recurring patterns and
execution differences between videos.

## Cross-round pattern synthesis

Synthesized from the "Patterns observed" / "Round-distinctive execution traits"
/ "Delivery & language patterns" sections of all 24 round files. Rows named below
are the Bayesian-ledger rows in `reference/factor-weights.md`; principles named
are from `reference/round-vision-framework.md`. "Clean/botched" verdicts are
execution judgments from the per-round analyses — **no judge RFD is on tape in
any recording**, so nothing here is outcome-calibrated.

### Recurring patterns, by ledger criterion

**1. Collapse & kick discipline (+0.8 one-voter / +0.9 path-of-least-resistance /
+0.6 decisive collapse) — the set's most consistently *clean* skill.**
Explicit, reasoned kicks appear in most rounds: round-06 drops three flows by
name with the turn-mooting reason stated; round-08 has symmetric early kicks
into a clean warming-vs-LNG endgame; round-02 shows rare *symmetric* discipline;
round-11 has kick discipline both ways; round-09's AFF kicks a whole flow by
conceding the opponent's own takeout card. The named set adds the sharpest
examples of **collapse *selection*** in the corpus: `tfa-state-doubles`'s NEG
keeps exactly the three skepticism warrants a God-framework structurally cannot
absorb (external world, linguistics, regress) and drops the three it can (evil
demon, Zeno, determinism), then narrows again in finals to linguistics alone —
choosing by **which of your own arguments the opponent has actually
neutralized**; `toc-2026-octos-emerald`'s NEG reduces three procedural sheets to
one filter (T) plus one standard (AI); `outreach-camp`'s NEG kicks the flow the
opponent straight-turned and names it ("we're not going for this DA").
The failure poles: round-15's NEG pivots twice across the back half; round-13's
AFF summary extends no case offense at all; round-07's PRO collapses decisively
but onto the flow carrying the opponent's heaviest ink. New failure modes:
`toc-2025-semis-iowacitywest`'s AFF **kicks its own advocacy in the final focus**
after extending it in summary (defensible only as a salvage), and
`king-rr-2026-finals`'s NEG rests on presumption in summary in a way its own
finals has to walk back. The well-vs-poorly split is *stated reasons and
cheapness*: clean collapses name what is being dropped and why the survivor is
the least-inked sufficient path.

**2. Cross-to-flow conversion (+0.4 clarification row, +0.7 indict row, and
their decay midpoints) — and the named set's genuine reversal of it.**
The numeric set's finding was that **cross output has a shelf life of one speech
unless re-executed on the flow** (round-09's signature result; round-10's card
indicts, round-08's EEZ press, round-11's two unconverted card presses all die
unflowed). The named progressive rounds **overturn this as a rule**: a single
crossfire answer routinely carries four speeches.
`king-rr-2026-finals`'s AFF extracts "would you read a racist argument if you
were sure you'd win?" — "**absolutely not**" — and re-runs it in every later
speech, using it in finals to lock the last speech out of solvency takeouts;
`toc-2025-semis-iowacitywest` does the same with "should racists lose?";
`toc-2026-semis-fremd-vs-nueva`'s NEG asks "**what have you done with the
policymaking education you've got?**" — a press only available because the aff
chose an *education* impact — and banks it in cross, summary, and finals;
`tfa-state-doubles`'s NEG **instantiates** its epistemic-regress warrant live
("P1 is 'God is objective.' Do you have P2?") rather than asserting it;
`toc-2026-octos-emerald`'s AFF extracts "how is that a warrant? Explain" and
re-runs it twice. The waste pole still exists — `outreach-camp`'s Pro wins a
command-F evidence indict and an impact-scenario mismatch and banks only the
first; `ukso-2025-semis`'s NEG pins a we-meet/fiat-illusory contradiction in
cross and converts only a compressed version. **The generalizable rule from the
full 24: cross wins convert when they target the impact or burden the opponent
chose, and expire when they target a peripheral card.**

**3. Weighing timing and interaction (+1.0 gap-filled-first, +0.7 comparative,
−0.5 wash, +0.6 full-chain timeframe).** Weighing that *interacts*: round-09's
timeframe race is actually raced; round-08 has engagement in both directions;
round-06 both teams answer the opposing mechanism explicitly; `harker-vs-strake`
and `outreach-camp` both stage try-or-die/timeframe races where the answer is a
uniqueness claim rather than a restated mechanism. Weighing that *washes*:
round-05's mirrored try-or-dies; round-12's paradigm war joined once, in the last
speech; round-11's CON; round-15's NEG's zero-weighing final focus. **The named
set's contribution is comparative weighing inside progressive rounds**, where it
is rarer and harder: `toc-2026-octos-emerald`'s NEG finals runs **three distinct
comparisons** (scope, internal-link strength, probability) against an AFF that
asserts a single filter three times without ever comparing it — the cleanest
instance in the corpus of the −0.7 "single meta-filter" row losing to the +0.7
"engages the opponent's weighing" row; `ukso-2025-semis`'s NEG answers a
prerequisite ROTB **on the K's own ethics** ("weighing anything but extinction is
the exact sacrificial logic you criticize") rather than restating impact
calculus; `toc-2025-octos-planowest`'s prefiat/postfiat clash genuinely
interacts. First-to-fill still wins: round-05 AFF, round-07 CON, round-15 AFF,
`outreach-camp` CON each file the frame early and ride it.

**4. Extension hygiene & back-half completeness (mirror row +0.6, safety nets
+0.6, skipped-speech −1.0).** "Final focus mirrors summary" is near-universal
discipline across all 24 files. Violations are round-deciding: round-13's AFF
leads finals on a turn absent from its own summary; round-09's NEG emboldenment
turn appears as a summary tag then vanishes; round-06's fisheries offense thins
to absence. The named set adds the **late-peaking turn** as a distinct failure:
`king-rr-2026-finals`'s NEG has a genuine link turn on the aff's ballot-solvency
card (ballots shape norms *toward disingenuous K-reading*) that is fleshed out
only in summary and finals, and the AFF flags the timing accurately;
`tfa-state-doubles`'s AFF finds the one argument that answers skepticism on its
own terms — **faith needs no evidence** — in the last thirty seconds of its
summary, after spending the speech re-extending the evidentiary case skepticism
had already neutralized. Both are the −0.7/−0.3 "hidden turn" row: the right
idea in the wrong speech. Safety-net breadth still marks strong teams (round-07,
round-13's NEG, round-12's CON, and `toc-2026-semis-fremd-vs-nueva`'s AFF, whose
**layer-independent weighing spike** — "we get to weigh the case even if we lose
the role of the ballot" — is extended in all three back-half speeches and is the
only reason it survives losing the framework layer).

**5. Evidence integrity: the unresolved central card dispute is the corpus's
most common *shared* failure (−0.6 row, charged to both sides).** Round-02
(Green postdate), round-05 ("filled with water"), round-07, round-09 (the ISR
turn litigated by dueling analytics across four speeches), round-12, round-15
all reach final focus with the pivotal card asserted both ways. The named set
supplies both the cleanest **executions** and the cleanest **counterexamples**:
`outreach-camp`'s Pro command-Fs the opponent's own link card in real time and
finds its "oil" localized to a domestic Chinese field; `harker-vs-strake`'s NEG
wins by noticing the AFF only ever *characterized* its terminal card;
`toc-2026-quarters-fremd`'s PRO forces the K's terminal card to be **read aloud**
in cross and argues the "500 years of war" claim is uncarded analysis — then,
unusually, **banks it** in summary and finals; `toc-2025-semis-iowacitywest`'s
NEG reads the AFF's own source text (the chains passage in *The Space Traders*)
back at it to argue the advocacy reifies the harm it names, which is the
proximate cause of the AFF's finals kick; `tfa-state-doubles`'s NEG turns the
aff's framework with the aff's **own doctrine** ("your evidence says hell is
avoided by belief, not by a ballot" — never answered). **The pattern across 24:
turning a position with its own text or its own commitments outperforms every
external indict, and is the single highest-leverage evidence move in the set.**

**6. Concession inflation (unwarranted-assertion row, −0.3 per instance) — the
back half's shared dialect, and it scales with technicality.** Claimed against a
contested record in round-02, round-09, round-10, round-11, and round-12. The
named progressive rounds run it at far higher density — roughly fifteen "they've
conceded" claims in a single four-minute summary in
`toc-2025-semis-iowacitywest`, roughly twenty in
`toc-2026-semis-fremd-vs-nueva`, and "they've dropped" anchoring a majority of
back-half sentences in `toc-2026-octos-emerald`. The consistent finding is
unchanged and sharpened: **real drops are round-winning (round-11's
broke-by-August, round-09's Sutterman/UUV, `toc-2026-octos-emerald`'s
skimming/control-F), and inflation at high density risks the judge discounting
the real drops alongside the manufactured ones.** In `ukso-2025-semis` the
inflation is not cosmetic at all — the ballot turns on whether a claimed
concession (the ROTB ordering) was genuinely conceded or merely
contested-without-out-warranting.

**7. Framing filters, presumption, and burden structures.** Top-shelf filters
discipline whole rounds until their edge cases: round-11's "only great-power war
is existential" filter holds 45 minutes and is then walked through; round-09's
probability filter is stolen through its own existential exception; round-13's
pre-fiat/post-fiat reframe filters the whole AFF case in one move. The named set
adds **burden architecture** as a distinct sub-skill. `tfa-state-doubles` is the
reference case: a truth-testing ROTB plus an **absolute-truth burden** converts
any surviving doubt into a ballot, so six *independent* cheap warrants beat an
opponent who must answer all six — and the AFF then **concedes truth-testing
while running a risk framing**, which is the round's decisive incompatibility
(a conceded burden requiring certainty cannot host offense that claims only
probability). `king-rr-2026-finals` shows the mirror-image hazard: a NEG summary
that closes "auto neg on presumption **at the very worst**" hands the AFF a
finals wedge ("so any 0.001% of methodological offense is an aff ballot"),
corrected one speech late — a live instance of the −0.8 presumption row's
gravitational pull. Presumption otherwise behaves as the ledger predicts:
round-13's NEG earns it off a genuinely dropped case; round-06's Team B brushes
the −0.8 pole.

**8. Crossfire conduct and round vision.** Gotcha hunting still backfires
(round-07, round-13, round-10), while link-cartography cross wins seed whole back
halves (round-09's guided walk-through, round-15's chain-walking,
`ukso-2025-semis`'s cyber-chain narration, `toc-2026-quarters-fremd`'s
anthropocentrism mapping). Preempt execution: round-06 answers a
cross-application one speech before it is made; round-09's AFF pre-answers an
evidence indict inside cross itself; and the named set's strongest instance is
`toc-2026-semis-fremd-vs-nueva`'s AFF, which **builds its entire anti-K
framework into the 1AC** before seeing the kritik (+0.9 front-half round vision)
— with the honest caveat that stacking eight unprioritized framework
sub-arguments let the NEG collapse to one filter and let the rest wash.
`toc-2026-octos-emerald` supplies the counter-lesson on pre-empting: its AFF
reads no-RVIs and competing-interps at length against an opponent who **runs no
RVI and concedes competing interps outright**, converting careful pre-emption
into a negative time trade (and costing it a contention, cut for time).

### 9. The kritik / progressive cluster (ten named rounds + round-13)

**Who runs the K, and what that changes.** The corpus now holds both postures.
**Negative kritiks**: `toc-2025-octos-planowest` (nuclear ontology),
`toc-2025-semis-planowest-vs-eastridge` (feminist/sexism),
`toc-2026-quarters-fremd` (flat ontology), `toc-2026-semis-fremd-vs-nueva`
(Derridean law), round-13 (securitization). **K-affs**:
`blake-quarters-2025` (Dialectic of Enlightenment), `ukso-2025-semis`
(becoming-woman), `king-rr-2026-finals` (Filipino melancholia),
`toc-2025-semis-iowacitywest` (afropessimism). The two postures generate
mirror-image rounds around **the same hinge**: an ordering fight over which layer
the judge starts at.

- **The highest-layer ordering fight is the recurring hinge, and the −0.8
  "conceded ordering" trap is almost always *avoided* — which is why so little
  resolves.** In `ukso-2025-semis`, `toc-2026-quarters-fremd`,
  `toc-2026-semis-fremd-vs-nueva`, and
  `toc-2025-semis-planowest-vs-eastridge`, **neither** side concedes the ordering:
  the K asserts a prerequisite, the opponent indicts the prerequisite as
  unpredictable, self-referential, or circular, and the question is litigated
  hardest in the **ungraded grand crossfire** and then re-run cold in both final
  focuses. The lesson is structural, not tactical: **when the hinge is an ordering
  question, cross is where it is understood and the last two speeches are where it
  must be won** — and in this corpus it usually is not won in either.
  `toc-2025-semis-iowacitywest` is the extreme case: with **no grand crossfire on
  tape at all**, the second crossfire carries the entire adversarial load, and
  whichever team already banked its cross material keeps the advantage.

- **The strongest kritiks link to the aff's own mechanism, not its impacts.**
  `toc-2025-octos-planowest`'s links are literally the aff's hawkish deterrence
  contentions; `toc-2026-quarters-fremd` links to the *solvency mechanism*
  (moving the decision from one human to another still assumes humans control
  weapons); `toc-2026-semis-fremd-vs-nueva` links because congressional
  authorization *is* an appeal to law. In all three the neg never has to *prove*
  a link — it reads the aff back to itself, which collapses the link debate and
  moves everything to framework. **The transferable aff lesson, stated in three
  separate files: a softer case denies the K its free links**, and neither policy
  aff in the 2026 set had a solvency story that avoided the critiqued
  institution.

- **"Clash turns the case" and "testing is prior to the charge" are the recurring
  anti-K-aff architecture.** `king-rr-2026-finals`'s NEG concedes the aff's values
  and argues its own model is the better vehicle for them ("only by debating it
  out can we use the skills to materialize your values in the real world");
  `toc-2025-semis-iowacitywest`'s NEG runs the same move plus "clash comes first
  because it is the means to determine whether the aff is true in the first
  place"; `toc-2026-quarters-fremd`'s NEG reaches the ontological version
  ("burden of proof comes before burden of rejoinder"). All three are the +0.8
  door-closing row: **block the ballot path even if the opponent wins its
  advocacy**, by claiming your layer is the only one capable of adjudicating.

- **K-aff solvency theories split three ways, and each invites a different
  attack.** `ukso-2025-semis` claims a **topical we-meet** and runs *fiat is
  illusory* — inviting the we-meet/advocacy-contradiction pin ("all your offense
  needs 'the EU is good' but you say fiat is illusory").
  `king-rr-2026-finals` is **openly non-topical** and grounds solvency in
  *ballots shape norms* — inviting ballot-solvency turns ("people will read the K
  disingenuously for competitive incentives") and the cheapest attack in the
  cluster, **"give them 30 speaker points instead"**: deny the *necessity* of the
  ballot rather than the merits of the advocacy.
  `toc-2025-semis-iowacitywest` builds **three independent routes**, only one of
  which is topic-dependent — which is what makes its mid-round **concession of
  the entire ontology debate** affordable ("our other arguments aren't contingent
  on ontology"), one sentence retiring a quarter of the opponent's constructive.

- **Prefiat objections leave postfiat claims conceded.** `ukso-2025-semis`'s AFF
  answers three extinction scenarios almost entirely as *representations* plus
  "fiat is illusory," never contesting the causal chains — the −0.7 row in close
  to pure form, and all-in on the ordering. `blake-quarters-2025` and
  `toc-2025-octos-planowest` show softer versions. The counterpoint is
  `tfa-state-doubles`'s **faith pivot**, the corpus's one example of answering an
  epistemic attack *on its own terms* rather than up-layering away from it.

- **Theory mechanics, consolidated.** `toc-2026-octos-emerald` is the reference
  file — a complete shell (interp → violation → five differentiated standards →
  voters → paradigm issues) read as an implementation, plus **T deployed against
  a theory shell** as an up-layer play. On **DTD vs. DTA** the corpus is better
  than the ledger's default: `blake-quarters-2025`'s "read it and lose" and
  `toc-2025-semis-planowest`'s specific exclusion warrant both push DTD from −0.6
  toward +0.3, and in `toc-2026-octos-emerald` **neither team ever argues DTA at
  all** — the NEG instead attacks DTD's *norm-setting mechanism* via a
  double-down/polarization argument, a more sophisticated attack than the default.
  On **RVIs**: read as a pre-empt in `toc-2026-octos-emerald` and never used.
  On **competing interps vs. reasonability**: conceded outright in
  `toc-2026-octos-emerald` to force a pure model-versus-model weighing round
  (+0.7 time trade) — which retroactively wasted the opponent's pre-empt blocks.

- **The circularity trap recurs, and it is always the same shape: frontlining a
  procedural with the very position the procedural indicts (−0.6).**
  `toc-2025-semis-iowacitywest`'s AFF answers T by arguing the T shell *proves*
  the kritik — and, worse, **leads with the circular version in finals while
  burying its carded counter-interp**. `toc-2026-octos-emerald`'s AFF answers T
  with "spirit over text" while its own shell is built on textual precision, and
  separately **demands the K pull specific quotes and then indicts the
  specificity it got**. In both files the opponent names the reversal on tape.

- **Progressive rounds are where the corpus's *best* cross conversion happens
  (see §2) and where its *worst* ordering resolution happens (see above).** The
  two findings are compatible and both worth teaching: material generated in
  cross transfers reliably when it targets the opponent's chosen burden; ordering
  disputes do not resolve because both sides treat them as filters rather than as
  claims requiring warranted comparison.

### 10. Delivery, register, and language (transcript-level)

**Standing caveat, and it is load-bearing.** Every delivery observation in this
corpus is **text and timestamps only** — the transcripts are offline
faster-whisper output with **no acoustic or prosody data whatsoever**. No file
makes a claim about tone, volume, emotion, or how anything sounded, and none
should. This matters most in `toc-2026-semis-fremd-vs-nueva`, where **one team
argues on the record that the other's constructive was too fast to flow** — the
transcript **cannot adjudicate that**, because its own garble is an ASR artifact
rather than evidence about the room, and the file says so explicitly.

- **Pace.** Legible back-half speeches across the named set cluster at roughly
  **170–240 WPM**. Fastest: `toc-2026-octos-emerald` (~220–240, pure theory) and
  `tfa-state-doubles` (~225–240, philosophy at spread). Mid: most K rounds at
  ~200–220. Slowest legible: `toc-2025-octos-planowest`'s NEG final focus at
  ~170. The one deliberate outlier is `outreach-camp`, whose organizers announced
  a **225 WPM cap** and sent constructive docs with analytics — and the analytic
  payoff is a legible back half where the decision turns on carded link warrants.
  **Constructives are essentially never reliably WPM-able** anywhere in the
  corpus; they outrun the ASR and are reported as fast-spread-and-corrupted, not
  measured.

- **Register splits are the corpus's most consistent delivery finding.** Nearly
  every kritik round shows a **two-register split**: dense theory vocabulary in
  the constructive (Braidotti/Deleuze in `ukso-2025-semis`; OOO/Deleuzian in
  `toc-2026-quarters-fremd`; Derridean in `toc-2026-semis-fremd-vs-nueva`;
  "nuclearity"/biopolitics in `toc-2025-octos-planowest`) versus plain technical
  register in the back half ("double bind," "impact calculus," "we-meet," "perm
  double bind," "terminal defense"). **The jargon is card text; the plain version
  is the debaters' own understanding** — and teams demonstrably operate both.
  Two files break the pattern in opposite directions: `king-rr-2026-finals` has a
  **three-register** split (literary/affective performance constructive →
  hyper-technical back half → colloquial crossfire), and
  `toc-2026-octos-emerald` has **no register shift at all** — a single procedural
  vocabulary sustained for 48 minutes, in which the only concrete nouns are
  *wiki*, *highlights*, *brackets*, *email chain*, and *ChatGPT*.

- **Warrant-substitute tics.** "**Obviously**" is the corpus's dominant one —
  flagged as a signature standing in for warrants in `harker-vs-strake`,
  `toc-2026-quarters-fremd`, `king-rr-2026-finals`,
  `toc-2026-semis-fremd-vs-nueva`, and `tfa-state-doubles`. Its companions:
  "**literally**" (`toc-2026-quarters-fremd`, `king-rr-2026-finals`),
  "**realize that**" (`toc-2025-semis-iowacitywest`), "**remember**" as a
  flow anchor (`king-rr-2026-finals`), and "**Look,**" opening sentences in the
  last three speeches across several files — the shared closing register of a
  tech round. `tfa-state-doubles` contributes a compact idiom used by **both**
  teams — "**clean**," "**game over**," and the formula "**drop the [X]
  evidence**" — meaning "this argument is conceded and dispositive."
  "**Conceded**" / "**they've dropped**" function as connective tissue
  everywhere, at their highest density in `toc-2025-semis-iowacitywest` and
  `toc-2026-octos-emerald`.

- **Signposting is the variable that decides whether a garbled round is
  analyzable at all.** Flow-level signposting (+0.4) is near-universal in the
  named set — speech order announced before nearly every back-half speech — and
  three files record it as *the only reason* the round is reconstructible under
  their ASR conditions (`toc-2026-octos-emerald`,
  `toc-2025-semis-iowacitywest`, `tfa-state-doubles`, the last of which
  signposts at the **warrant** level, re-citing arguments by name rather than
  position, in every speech). `toc-2026-semis-fremd-vs-nueva` goes further and
  shows roadmaps being **negotiated aloud** between opponents before a speech
  ("where do you start flowing?").

- **Recurring rhetorical figures.** A single portable analogy repeated verbatim
  across a round is a recognizable habit: the "drop my phone" consequentialism
  analogy three times in `blake-quarters-2025`, and a presumption name-example
  three times in `toc-2026-semis-fremd-vs-nueva` — in the latter case attached to
  the team's most fragile argument.

- **ASR artifacts must never be scored as delivery.** Every file records the
  distinction explicitly: looping repetitions ("energy energy energy,"
  "under under under," "different different different," "extended extended
  extended," "policy policy policy," "it really does" ×3) are **model stutter on
  fast or overlapping audio, not debater repetition**. Two files also flag
  outright hallucination or unrecoverable stretches (round-09's courtroom
  hallucination run; `toc-2025-semis-iowacitywest`'s multiple unrecoverable
  passages) and treat them as unusable rather than as round content.

- **Crossfire conduct.** Most of the corpus sits below the −0.3 condescension
  threshold. The row is **most clearly triggered** in
  `toc-2026-octos-emerald` (pointed personal exchanges and an interruption
  dispute, notably in a round where one team's standards are about
  accessibility). Heated-but-argument-directed exchanges occur in
  `king-rr-2026-finals`'s grand cross and `toc-2025-semis-iowacitywest` — in both,
  the teams steer identity-adjacent disputes back toward **models rather than
  persons** when they escalate. In-speech **profanity** appears as register in
  three named files and is never directed at opponents on tape; it is recorded as
  a text-level fact, not a conduct finding. The most civil set — and the only
  genuinely funny one — is `tfa-state-doubles`, whose grand crossfire converts an
  abstract certainty claim into a **betting hypothetical** and closes on a joke.

- **Context audible on tape.** The named rounds capture scaffolding the numeric
  set hid: a camp tech-demo's announced speed cap and doc-sharing
  (`outreach-camp`); **pre-round airport/travel logistics**
  (`king-rr-2026-finals`); a **pre-round no-talking protocol**
  (`toc-2025-semis-iowacitywest`); **flex prep used substantively as flow
  repair** (`toc-2026-octos-emerald`); and two live disruptions — a **mid-round
  room evacuation for hotel capacity** and a **speech-drop file deletion** — in
  `toc-2026-semis-fremd-vs-nueva`, **neither of which either team converted into
  a procedural argument**, which is itself the clearest example in the corpus of
  disruption handled without escalation.

### Execution differentiators: strongest vs weakest in the set

The strongest execution observed — round-11's PRO, round-09's AFF, round-12's
CON, round-06 (both teams), and from the named set
`toc-2026-octos-emerald`'s NEG (concede every paradigm issue, force a pure
model comparison, then run three distinct weighing claims in finals),
`toc-2026-semis-fremd-vs-nueva`'s NEG (a trilemma with no exposed horn, plus an
opponent's own concession repurposed as the controlling frame), and
`tfa-state-doubles`'s NEG (collapse selection by structural compatibility) —
shares three habits: **every cross win is re-executed on the flow within one
speech, weighing engages the opponent's mechanism by name, and one strategy runs
unbroken from second rebuttal to final focus.** The weakest — round-13's AFF,
round-15's NEG, round-10's PRO, and from the named set the recurring pattern of
**late-peaking turns** (`king-rr`'s disingenuous-K turn, `tfa-state-doubles`'s
faith pivot) and **superseded-argument answering**
(`toc-2026-semis-fremd-vs-nueva`'s AFF answering the rebuttal-era perm answer
instead of the operative prerequisite claim) — fails on exactly those axes,
usually while ahead on raw material. **The gap is conversion, continuity, and
timing, not argument quality.**

### Register/context spread and ASR caveats

All 24 recordings are Public Forum, but the named set widened the register
substantially. **Format spread:** fast-circuit/TOC-style tech remains dominant;
`outreach-camp` is a capped, doc-sent camp tech-demo; round-12 is a championship
final with the ceremony on tape; round-08 and round-13 read as practice/scrims;
round-13, round-15, and `ukso-2025-semis` are online rounds with doc-share
logistics audible. **There is still no true lay or slow round in the set** —
lay-adaptation rows remain unexercised, with `outreach-camp`'s announced 225 WPM
cap the only throttled speech anywhere.

**Argument-type spread:** thirteen substantive policy rounds; **ten kritik /
framework / theory rounds** plus round-13; one **pure disclosure-theory** round
with no substantive clash at all (`toc-2026-octos-emerald`); one
**philosophy/skepticism** round that is functionally LD in a PF shell
(`tfa-state-doubles`).

**Topic spread:** encryption/NDAA politics (round-02); US–China minerals and
resource extraction in six variants (round-05, round-06, round-07, round-13,
round-15, `harker-vs-strake`, `outreach-camp`, `blake-quarters-2025`); nuclear
energy three times (round-08, round-12, `toc-2025-semis-iowacitywest`); Taiwan
support (round-09); border surveillance (round-10); UNSC veto (round-11);
**war powers three times and cross-corroborated** (`king-rr-2026-finals`,
`toc-2026-semis-fremd-vs-nueva`, `toc-2026-octos-emerald`); **institutional
investors / single-family homes twice, same tournament**
(`tfa-state-semis`, `tfa-state-doubles`); UK rejoining the EU (`ukso-2025-semis`);
judicial review / the political question doctrine (`toc-2026-quarters-fremd`).
Where two or three independent files land on the same resolution, the topic
label is high-confidence; elsewhere it is **inferred, not verbatim**.

**ASR caveat, uniform across files:** constructives and rebuttals are heavily
garbled at card-reading speed (an artifact, never a delivery finding), so
content-level claims rest mostly on crossfires, summaries, grand crossfires, and
final focuses. The named progressive rounds are **worse than the numeric set**,
because the decisive layer is usually the most corrupted text: the kritik itself.
`toc-2025-semis-iowacitywest` has the worst transcript in the corpus (multiple
unrecoverable passages; all pre-crossfire analysis is structural only), and in
`toc-2026-octos-emerald` there is almost **no card text to recover at all** — the
round is analytics-driven, and its shell architecture is reconstructible only
because both teams re-cite it by name throughout the back half. **Card authors in
the named set are inferred and low-confidence nearly everywhere**, with a handful
of conceptually-secure exceptions (Derrick Bell's *Space Traders*; Derrida's
*Force of Law*; the Braidotti, Bryant/Harman, and Unger traditions).
Several files additionally flag that **school-to-side attribution is not
verifiable from the audio** (`toc-2026-semis-fremd-vs-nueva`,
`toc-2026-octos-emerald`, `harker-vs-strake`) and use AFF/NEG only.

**No recording includes a judge RFD**, so this synthesis grades execution
against the ledger, not against outcomes. Two files carry additional handling
notes worth restating: `toc-2025-semis-planowest-vs-eastridge` and
`king-rr-2026-finals` analyze identity-adjacent strategy clinically without
editorializing on the underlying social claims, and
`toc-2025-semis-iowacitywest` documents **unverified in-round allegations about
tournament and judge conduct**, redacts all personal names, and explicitly takes
no position on their truth.
