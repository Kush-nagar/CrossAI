Primary resources, also known as natural resources, occur naturally and are derived from the environment in order to process them. Apart from resources such as water, air, sunlight, forest area or agricultural land, which exist as separate entities, other resources such as metals ores and fossil fuels have to be extracted from the soil to make them usable. Their value is predominantly determined by the relative scarcity of the resource in combination with its exploitability for industrial use. In modern industry, most natural resources are used as raw materials from which upstream materials and finished products are produced. As primary resources for energy and industrial production, they are a vital requirement of any economic activity.
China's natural resources
<mark>With a</mark> total <mark>surface area of</mark> approximately <mark>9.6 million square kilometers</mark>, <mark>China has abundant land resources</mark> and possesses strategic shares in various mineral resources. Due to its geologic preconditions, China has <mark>proved reserves of nearly all major minerals</mark>. As of 2023, proven reserves of iron ore in China had amounted to almost 17 billion tons. But <mark>due to the low</mark> quality of its <mark>iron</mark> ore <mark>reserves</mark>, the demand of iron ore in <mark>China</mark> still heavily <mark>relies on</mark> iron ore <mark>imports</mark>.
The domestic economy in China is largely based on energy-intensive heavy industrial production. In order to maintain its growth momentum, the country has subsequently increased its primary energy production to over nearly five billion tons of coal equivalent as of 2024. <mark>China’s north-western regions</mark> such as Inner Mongolia and Xinjiang, the so-called Chinese coal belt, not only <mark>produce huge amount of coal</mark> but also <mark>petroleum and natural gas</mark>. <mark>China invests billions yuan in</mark> geological exploration of resources every year, predominantly oil and gas. Among non-fossil resources, <mark>uranium and gold</mark> received the highest investment for geological exploration, around a billion yuan annually.
