2AC – BRACKETING
Interpretation: Debaters must read the original text for all of their cards.
Violation: Goertzel 17
We’ll pre-empt “harmful language” / “grammatical error” objections here: a) making exceptions to the interp justifies a slippery slope that doesn’t solve our offense b) if the evidence is bad or has harmful language don’t read it in the first place
Standards:
1. Argument Depth – forcing debaters to cut evidence that actually says what they want leads to more research and in depth argument testing rather than making up fake warrants and args
2. Evidence Ethics – bracketing creates an incentive to stretch their evidence, making up quotes, and destroying the constitutive purpose of debate. also creates spillover incentives to miss-cut evidence and change the entire meaning of articles. us checking back doesn’t solve: a) time and prep skews us because we have to shift through all their ev which shouldn’t be our burden which bites the link b) they can just shift out and say “not what we meant” or “author actually meant X.”
Fairness is a voter: a) a game with rules requires equal access and potential to get the ballot b) fairness is a meta-constraint on your ability to evaluate the round, otherwise you actively hack against them
Education is a voter: a) constitutive purpose of debate and why school fund it b) only terminal impact and tangible skill we get from debate
Drop the debater: a) deters future abuse because not worth the loss b) punish them for the violation and rectify time lost on theory c) avoid the timeskew from baiting and spamming abusive arguments
No rvis: a) you don’t win for being fair it’s illogical b) encourages baiting and scripting theory which chills checking abuse
Competing interps: a) reasonability encourages a race to the margins of what is reasonable b) don’t know what rule to follow because they don’t set a clear standard
Use a norm setting model: a) solves long term for future rounds and potential abuse whereas in-round abuse only matters once 2. in round abuse is arbitrary and is a question of whether the violation is “reasonable” or unresolvable questions of what ground we lost which invites judge intervention.
