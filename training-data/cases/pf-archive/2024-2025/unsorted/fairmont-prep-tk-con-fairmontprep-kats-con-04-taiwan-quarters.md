### Embedded weighing

Cards such as Ross 24 ("Food Insecurity Tied to Shorter Life, Early Death") and Chan 22 ("Millions... marching towards starvation") present large-scale impacts but lack comparative language ("outweighs," "first," "before the aff can solve") that would allow them to function as pre-written weighing in the back half. ### Offensive spikes
