Writers like Robert Kaplan argue that South Asia's importance will continue to grow, its future shaped by the competition

and massive trade with Asia, and has been a central player in the region's security system

arrangement, this is the region where disengagement or a recession of American power would have the most far-reaching

provocative. This could lead the other leading powers of the region close to China—particularly Japan, South Korea and

protracted internal conflicts that cause humanitarian disasters and provide operating space for extremists (the Syria
model); the further proliferation of nuclear weapons; the seizure of a state or part of a
state by extremists that then use the territory they control to support transnational
terrorism; and the old specter of major war between nations. U.S. political leaders and security

in the summer of 1914. Power balances work best when one key state is able to shift sides to preserve the balance, but there
