### Internal consistency
**ABSENT:** The overview (Wilson 24, Ferragamo 24) frames Somaliland as a *democratic success story* deserving recognition on merit, while the Economic Development contention (Idaan 24, Al-Ghwell 24) frames recognition as a *tool to unlock investment* for a struggling economy. These narratives tension: if Somaliland is already a "viable political system" with "dynamic private enterprise" (Wilson 24), the urgency of "economic isolation nullifies growth" (Idaan 24) is undercut. A bridging card — e.g., "Democratic consolidation requires economic delivery; without recognition, poverty erodes legitimacy" — would unify the framing.
