By all accounts, <mark>2024 was a turning point</mark> in the fight to hold Israel, its politicians, military and government officials accountable for its violations of international law. Last year started with <mark>South Africa</mark> <mark>submi</mark>tting its <mark>case to</mark> the international court of justice (<mark>ICJ</mark>) in January, accusing Israel of committing acts of genocide in Gaza.
