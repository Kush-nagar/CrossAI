That said, the run-up to the vote was far from smooth, due to rising internal tensions and an unresolved conflict in the east. Over the last three decades it has developed many of the trappings of a state, including its own currency, security forces and civilian administration in the capital, Hargeisa. Even so, Mogadishu rejects Somaliland’s independence and no country has recognised it. At the same time, its reputation for orderly polls and relatively consensual politics has come under strain recently, and disputes among politicians caused a two-year delay of the presidential vote. ¶ In the end, Somaliland’s institutions and political establishment largely withstood the stress test.
