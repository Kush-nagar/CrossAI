### Warrant depth

- **Warranted:** Mirilovic & Siroky 15 provides a full causal mechanism: "recognition decisions may send a relatively clear signal to domestic audiences... countries facing such a threat will be less likely to recognize an aspiring state for fear of setting a precedent that would embolden aggrieved groups at home" — this *is* the warrant for the Pandora's Box tag.  
- **Warranted:** ICG 24 explains *how* Al-Shabaab exploits the dispute: "portrayed itself as the only actor capable of rolling back Ethiopian ambitions... depicting the Somali federal government as too weak... win new recruits based on this narrative, something it has done in the past."

- **Claim+cite only:** Hassan 21 tag "Somalis as people do not easily forget or forgive those who stab them in the back... deep resentment is bound to poison relations for a long time" — no warrant for why this resentment translates to Al-Shabaab recruitment or state failure.  
- **Claim+cite only:** Aliyev 16 tag "Increased terrorism would create a failed state" — the card studies VNSAs broadly and finds pro-regime veto players matter more than civil war; it does not establish that *terrorism specifically* causes state failure in Somalia's context.
