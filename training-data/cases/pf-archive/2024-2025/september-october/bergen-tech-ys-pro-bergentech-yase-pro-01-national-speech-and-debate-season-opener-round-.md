As globalization deepens, cross-border trade is increasingly essential for economic growth. However, efficient trade often hinges on robust border security measures. According to the World Trade Organization (WTO), secure borders
are a prerequisite for stable and lawful cross-border trade. Trade efficiency—determined by ease, speed, cost, and predictability—can significantly boost a nation's economic development.** Border security
regulates and monitors cross-border movements to ensure national safety. Its benefits extend
beyond preventing illicit activities and illegal immigration to fostering secure trade channels,which boost investor confidence and stimulate economic growth. Current strategies
vary globally, influenced by geography, socio-economic factors, and political dynamics. Border security shapes the trade ecosystem. While stringent controls prevent
security threats, overly strict measures can hinder trade. The 2019 U.S.-Mexico border dispute exemplifies this, where increased security caused delays and significant economic losses. Efficient border controls, however, can
streamline trade, reduce costs, and foster a conducive business environment.
