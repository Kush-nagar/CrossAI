### Internal consistency
Consistent framing: Both contentions argue *status quo (state regulation) works* and *federal framework breaks it*. Contention 1: state legal markets already shrinking illegal markets (AGA 20); federal regulation reverses this (Minton 18, Shancer 17). Contention 2: state tax revenue funds services (Simon 25, Spitz 20); federal framework cuts states out (Spitz 20) and enables loopholes (Young 25). No contention undercuts another. Uniform "state experimentation good / federal one-size-fits-all bad" narrative. Only minor tension: Bonny-Noach 24 (Israeli data) suggests illegal gamblers have *higher* participation in sports betting (45.3% vs 21.5%), which could imply legal markets don't fully capture demand — but case uses it only for harm severity (problem gambling 15% vs 2%), not participation rates, so no direct contradiction.
