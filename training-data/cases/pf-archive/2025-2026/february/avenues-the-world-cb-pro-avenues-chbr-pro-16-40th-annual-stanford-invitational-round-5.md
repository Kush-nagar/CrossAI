Justice Alito’s majority opinion in Murphy offered three key reasons why sports betting belongs in the states’ jurisdiction rather than the federal government’s: (1) to curb the federal government’s power, (2) to foster greater political accountability to the state governments, and (3) to prevent a scenario where Congress is not held responsible for the financial consequences of its decisions.
