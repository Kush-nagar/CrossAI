Price Volatility One of the most immediate economic effects of mineral scarcity is price volatility. When demand outstrips supply, prices tend to
rise. This can have cascading effects throughout the economy, particularly for industries that rely on these minerals as essential inputs. For instance,
consider the electric vehicle (EV) market. Lithium is a critical component of EV batteries. As demand for EVs has surged, so has the price of
lithium. This increase in the price of a key input drives up the overall cost of EVs, potentially slowing down their adoption rate. Price volatility is
also detrimental for long-term investment in mining projects, as uncertainty about future prices makes it difficult to assess the profitability of
new ventures.
