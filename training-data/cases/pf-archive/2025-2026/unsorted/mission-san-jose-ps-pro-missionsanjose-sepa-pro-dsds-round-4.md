The youth of America are unready to join the economy
Tia 25 finds financial literacy rates among Gen Z are the lowest of any generation in recorded history putting them at a disadvantage in areas like saving, investing, and risk comprehension.
Reuters 25 pins the cause: sports betting.
Youth are the most profitable base for sports gambling companies. Youth enter the market years before their brain develops, thus placing them at a high risk to get addicted to gambling.

Indeed, Isidore 25 finds that online gambling companies jump at the opportunity, signing multibillion dollar deals with leagues with monopoly level restrictions.

Goldstein 22 warns that these companies weaponize vast pools of consumer data, mapping behavioral patterns to systematically target individuals most vulnerable to gambling addiction. (Cornell Journal of Law and Public Policy 25)

Sports leagues aren’t shy of cashing in as well. Cohen 26 reports sports leagues have pivoted to increasing youth engagement, turning to social media presence, Netflix, Roblox, ABC Mouse, and Nickelodeon.

Vulnerable minds paired with corrupting influence brew a vicious poison.
In a metastudy of 54 research papers, Eduardo 23 finds these practices are massive statistical predictors of high frequency sports betting.

Hundreds of millions poured into advertising have corrupted the youth, as Bland 24 reports 67% of students at college bet on sports at alarmingly high rates.

Crucially, online sports betting devastates the economy in two ways

The first is crippling credit
When gamblers enter the market, hoping to make extra cash as a quick side hustle. Adamcyzc 25 finds that they see no difference between gambling and investment, even though Boyd 25 finds that 95% of gamblers lose money when betting.
After a loss, Adamcyzc finds that gamblers want to make their losses back. Thus, to gain instant satisfaction, they bet larger and larger, falling into a downward spiral of debt.
This is made even easier because the user’s credit card is loaded directly onto the app and people do not begin to realize when they are betting money that isn't theirs.
With less capital, Anderson 24 finds every dollar in sports betting is two dollars not put into the stock market leading to a net 14% decrease in investment.
Problematically investment is the core of a capitalist economy, Ilsley 24 finds investment in the stock market allows business to increase innovation, expand operations, and pay off debt.
Mckinney 23 quantifies 50% of American economic growth is contingent on innovation.

The second is risk perception

Sports betting companies take advantage of youth psychology as Pempus 25 warns sports betting monopolies are incorporating the same highly addictive methods as social media companies. These tactics are empirically dangerous as the vast majority of Gen Z finds them addicting.

In a meta-analysis of over a hundred fifty studies from the National Library of Medicine in 2023 finds sports betting is associated with increased impulsivity, sensation seeking, and decreased long term thinking.

This biological shift is a death sentence for financial security. Because problem gambling is positively associated with high portfolio turnover, addicts treat the stock market like a casino, Mosenhaur 21 finds killing 3.8% in annual returns. Worse, when gamblers face market losses their screwed prefrontal cortex triggers loss chasing behavior, leading them to stop stable investment and go back to gambling platforms.
The youth of today will invest into the future of tomorrow.
As populations grow older they invest substantially more into the stock market. The Motley Fool 25 confirms 60% of total investors in the market are gen X and millennials

Dan Hunt of Morgan Stanley in 2025 finds risky investing increases panic selling, favorability to short term investments and decreases portfolio diversification.
BFAM 25 warns these behaviors increase the risk of market crashes.
The United Nations in 2010 warns a recession of this level would cause 100 million people to fall into poverty.
