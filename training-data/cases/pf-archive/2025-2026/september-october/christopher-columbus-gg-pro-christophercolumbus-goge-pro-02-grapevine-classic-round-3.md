## Construction-criteria audit

### Embedded weighing
**ABSENT:** No card explicitly weighs Scotland independence impacts against Green Tech impacts (magnitude, probability, timeframe). The case reads as two independent contentions without a meta-weighing mechanism. A filling card would carry a tag like "Weighing — Scotland secession risk outweighs green cooperation on magnitude & probability (nuclear destabilization + £20bn+ fiscal shock vs. incremental CCUS gains)" and sit at the top of the case or as a standalone overview.

### Offensive spikes
**ABSENT:** No cards preempt likely Con positions such as "EU would block/veto UK rejoining," "Rejoining requires Euro adoption," "UK loses budget rebate," or "Sovereignty/immigration backlash." An offensive spike would be a card tagged "EU accession treaty allows UK opt-outs on Euro & rebate — rebuttal to sovereignty/cost DA" or "Polling shows UK public supports rejoining even with conditions — turns Con's democracy argument."

### Defensive spikes
**ABSENT:** No embedded frontlines against Con responses like "Scotland independence unlikely without Westminster consent," "CCUS unproven at scale," "TCA renegotiation solves green cooperation without rejoining," or "UK can bilateral with EU on energy." A defensive spike would be a card tagged "Section 30 order politically inevitable if SNP wins majority — Watt 14 proves Westminster concedes referendums under pressure" placed immediately after the Scotland contention, or "CCUS commercial projects operational in Norway & Netherlands — CITP 25 evidence confirms tech readiness" after the Carbon Storage subpoint.

### Evidence quality
**ABSENT:** Most sources lack author qualifications, institutional credibility markers, or dataset specifics. "The Week 25'" is a magazine summary without methodology; "Scottish Government 24'" and "23'" are self-published advocacy; "Walker 25" and "Lockhart 25'" are journalists citing polls without sample sizes; "Ibrahim 21" is a Foreign Policy op-ed by a think-tank director but no peer review; "Ecologist 25" and "CITP 25" have no author names or peer-review status; "UK Parliament 25" is a committee report but cited as "No Author"; "Dupont 21" is a 2021 journal article (outdated for 2025 topic). A winning evidence-comparison card would be: "OBR 2024 Fiscal Risks Report — Brexit 4% GDP hit confirmed by independent fiscal watchdog with 40-year modeling" or "European Commission 2025 Enlargement Report — UK rejoining assessment timeline & conditions."

No card addresses this interaction. A consistency card would be tagged "Scottish EU accession separate from UK — UK rejoining preserves North Sea assets & Faslane under UK sovereignty" or "SNP independence timeline post-2026 allows UK-EU green integration first."
