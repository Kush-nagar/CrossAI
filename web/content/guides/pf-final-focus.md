---
title: How to give a Public Forum final focus
description: The last 2 minutes of the round: mirror your summary, weigh, and write the judge's ballot for them.
order: 5
---

Final focus is 2 minutes, and it's the last thing the judge hears. It should sound like the reason for decision you want them to write.

## Mirror the summary

Final focus goes for the same argument as your partner's [summary](/guides/pf-summary), in nearly the same words. Anything new here is either ignored or held against you. If summary collapsed to contention one, final focus goes for contention one.

## A simple structure

1. **The ballot story in one sentence.** "We win because their only offense is non-unique and ours is the bigger, faster impact."
2. **Your offense, extended.** The same link chain summary used.
3. **Why it outweighs.** Comparative [weighing](/guides/weighing-in-debate) against their specific argument.
4. **Why their offense doesn't matter.** The defense that summary extended, and why it's enough.

## Write the ballot for the judge

Use the words you'd want in their decision: "Prefer our timeframe weighing because theirs needs three steps to happen and ours is already underway." Judges who are tired at the end of a round often vote for whoever made the decision easiest.

## For lay judges

Slow down, drop the jargon, and tell a story: what happens in the world where they vote for you, and what happens if they don't. One clear impact beats three technical ones.

## Common mistakes

- Breaking with summary.
- Reading new cards or new responses.
- Spending the whole speech on defense with no reason to vote *for* you.
- Rushing the weighing into the last ten seconds.
